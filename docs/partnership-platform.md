# Human Bond as a Partnership Operating System

Human Bond is a simple place for two or more humans to form and manage an ongoing partnership. Marriage is one of the most important templates, but the same primitive can support a household, family enterprise, professional partnership, small business, co-ownership arrangement, or project team.

The product insight is that many partnerships already exist in practice before they exist in an institution. Two people decide to build a life, raise a family, run a business, buy property, or create income together. The legal and financial machinery that should make that relationship legible and manageable is difficult, expensive, fragmented, and often inaccessible.

## Marriage is often an economic partnership

A marriage is not only a romantic or domestic relationship. It is frequently also:

- a shared economic unit;
- a household treasury;
- a joint investment and savings vehicle;
- a business partnership;
- a risk-sharing arrangement;
- a long-term governance relationship.

Often one spouse earns most or all of the visible income while the other contributes unpaid care, household management, relationship support, property management, or work that makes the income possible. The income should not be modeled as belonging only to the person whose name appears on the pay stub. The legal treatment varies by jurisdiction and marital regime, but many systems recognize some form of marital, community, or jointly managed economic interest. Human Bond should make those contributions and decisions visible without pretending to replace local law.

A Human Bond can therefore represent a marriage as a shared partnership with:

- contribution records that do not reduce value to cash income;
- agreed ownership or beneficial-share policies;
- joint and individual accounts;
- household and business budgets;
- treasury approvals;
- savings and investment policies;
- compensation and distribution rules;
- exit, separation, death, and dissolution procedures;
- selective disclosures to banks, advisers, counterparties, or services.

The system must not decide a couple’s legal property regime. It records what the parties agree, makes assumptions explicit, and points to jurisdiction-specific legal advice where required.

## Why ordinary partnerships are hard in real life

A partnership may be economically substantial but administratively informal. To operate it properly, the partners may need to:

1. choose a legal form;
2. incorporate or register an entity where appropriate;
3. draft bylaws, a partnership agreement, operating agreement, or marital agreement;
4. obtain legal and tax advice;
5. open a bank account that a financial institution will accept;
6. establish signing authority and spending limits;
7. account for income, expenses, taxes, reimbursements, and distributions;
8. manage savings and investments;
9. decide how new debt or commitments are approved;
10. preserve records and evidence of decisions;
11. plan for incapacity, disagreement, exit, succession, and dissolution.

These steps are especially difficult for a small partnership. Professional advice is expensive. Banks are slow and jurisdiction-specific. Forms and institutions are designed around companies or individuals, not the actual human relationship that is doing the work.

Human Bond does not eliminate legal requirements, taxes, banking, or fiduciary duties. It gives the partners a coherent operating layer before, alongside, or in some cases as a bridge toward those institutions.

## The Human Bond operating layer

```text
Verified humans
      ↓
Human-readable charter / bylaws / partnership agreement
      ↓
Partner Agents: one agent for each human
      ↓
Partnership Agent: one agent for the formed partnership
      ↓
Agreement policy and approvals
      ↓
Treasury, accounts, payments, savings, investments, and records
      ↓
Optional legal entity, lawyer, bank, accountant, or regulated provider
```

The legal entity, if one is needed, is an external legal wrapper. The Human Bond remains an agreement among humans. A company, DAO, trust, bank, or other institution may be connected as a service provider, asset holder, counterparty, or execution venue, but it is not a Human Bond member.

## Partnership lifecycle

### Formation

The partners verify their unique humanity, select a template, disclose their goals and constraints, and negotiate a human-readable charter. The charter can function as operating rules, bylaws, a partnership agreement, a household agreement, or a marital agreement depending on context and legal review.

### Activation

After all required humans approve, NEAR creates the Agreement and its Partnership Agent. The Agreement records the charter commitment, members, roles, shares, authority, treasury policy, amendment rule, and exit path.

### Operation

The Partner Agents represent individual interests. The Partnership Agent coordinates the accepted rules. Together they can:

- prepare budgets;
- route income and payments;
- classify expenses;
- enforce spending thresholds;
- request approvals;
- track contributions and distributions;
- manage TIME purchases and offers;
- propose savings allocations;
- prepare investment decisions;
- maintain decision and treasury records;
- produce selective disclosures for approved third parties.

Agents propose and coordinate. The Agreement policy and required human approvals authorize consequential actions.

### Institutional connection

A partnership can connect to lawyers, accountants, banks, payment providers, investment platforms, tax services, and legal entities. Human Bond can package the agreed charter and decision history for those institutions, but it does not claim that an on-chain agreement alone opens a bank account, creates a corporation, or satisfies a jurisdiction’s legal formalities.

### Change and dissolution

Every partnership needs a way to change its rules and to end. The Agreement defines amendment thresholds, individual exit, separation, buyout, death or incapacity procedures, debt and treasury treatment, asset distribution, and final dissolution. The Partnership Agent enters a winding-down state and cannot create new obligations outside the approved process.

## Legal and professional boundary

Human Bond is protocol and operating infrastructure, not a law firm, bank, accountant, investment adviser, or licensed fiduciary. It should:

- make legal and financial assumptions explicit;
- identify when a lawyer, accountant, tax adviser, bank, or regulated investment provider is needed;
- support jurisdiction-specific templates without presenting one template as universal law;
- preserve signed versions, approvals, and amendment history;
- distinguish an agreement between humans from a legal entity’s rights and obligations;
- never imply that a registry response is legal advice or a substitute for due diligence.

## Product thesis

Human Bond makes the basic unit of economic cooperation legible:

> Two or more humans can form a trusted operating agreement, manage shared resources, make decisions, and connect to the institutions they need—without first having to become experts in corporate, banking, legal, and administrative machinery.

Marriage is not an exception to this thesis. It is one of its clearest and most economically important examples.
