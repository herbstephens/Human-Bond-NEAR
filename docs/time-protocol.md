# TIME Protocol

**1 TIME = 1 hour of verified human work.**

TIME Protocol is the economic layer for valuing, recording, and distributing verified human time. NEAR is the canonical home for its contracts and assets.

## Components

- human verification registry;
- one-human-one-calendar rules;
- work receipts and completion verification;
- TIME fungible token;
- marketplace and agreement settlement;
- commons distribution;
- governance and treasury controls.

## Critical distinction

World ID establishes unique human identity. It does not prove work. TIME therefore requires a separate work-verification protocol with counterparty approvals, evidence commitments, dispute windows, anti-collusion controls, and revocation rules.

## Relationship to Human Bond

Human Bond supplies the agreement layer for work and collaboration. TIME supplies the accounting and value layer for verified human work. A Human Bond agreement may specify contributions in hours, TIME, fiat-equivalent value, or another permitted asset.
