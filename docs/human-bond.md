# Human Bond

Human Bond is an agreement protocol for two or more verified humans. It makes collaboration explicit, private, executable, and easier to leave cleanly.

## Templates

- **Marriage:** two members, often equal shares, optional exclusivity, soulbound membership, explicit dissolution.
- **Business partnership:** contribution shares, treasury permissions, vesting, buyout, and dispute rules.
- **Co-ownership:** ownership percentages, expenses, use rights, sale and exit terms.
- **Project team:** roles, milestones, compensation, revenue split, and completion rules.
- **Care agreement:** responsibilities, schedules, expenses, emergency authority, and review terms.

Templates are product presets over one general Agreement primitive. The protocol is not a marriage app.

## Agent role

Each human member has a Partner Agent that represents their stated interests and constraints. After the Bond is formed, a separate Partnership Agent represents the collective agreement. The Partner Agents negotiate and seek their principals’ approvals; the Partnership Agent coordinates the accepted charter, operations, monitoring, and authorized registry disclosures. Neither agent type can impersonate a human, prove humanity, change terms without authorization, reveal private Bond data, or move treasury funds outside the agreement policy. The Partnership Agent is not a human member.

## Registry

After a Bond is created, its status may be queryable by a paid third-party registry client under explicit consent and disclosure policy. A client such as Tinder may pay for a minimal answer to a specific claim—such as whether an active exclusive marriage-type Bond exists—without receiving the partner’s identity, charter, or private terms. The registry is consent-based and metered, not a public relationship database.
